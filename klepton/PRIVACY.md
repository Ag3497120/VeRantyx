# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

This policy covers the **HL1VR**, **HL2VR**, and **P1VR** applications for Apple
Vision Pro, which are launchers for the open-source Klepton runtime.

## What we collect

Nothing. These apps do **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
data.

- No account, login, or sign-up is required.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising identifiers are used.
- No data is sent to us or to any third party.

## How the apps work

The apps run entirely on your device. Game content is loaded from files that
**you** provide locally on the device; the apps do not require a network
connection to function. Any files you select stay on your device.

## Children's privacy

Because the apps collect no data at all, they collect no data from children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
