- **Sharper graphics.** Textures are more detailed, and your Vision Pro now uses all the graphics settings Glovebox picked for it.
- **Faster start.** Your saves are ready sooner when you press Play, and you can see how far along they are.
- **Your settings travel with your saves.** Choices like how you move now carry over between your PC and your Vision Pro.
- **Also:** controls for left-handed players (set Weapon Hand to Left in the game's options), a way to send us a problem report from your Vision Pro (tap your name, then Share Diagnostics), and fixes for sign-in, downloads and building on some Macs.

**How to update**
1. On your Mac, open Glovebox and choose **Glovebox › Check for Updates…**, then click **Install Update**.
2. When Glovebox opens again, keep your Vision Pro nearby and awake, and click **Install Update** to put the new Glovebox: HLA on it. Your downloaded game data and saves stay on your headset.
